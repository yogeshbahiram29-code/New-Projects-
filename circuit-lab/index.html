Ideas to try

  • Put two bulbs in series, then in parallel. Which arrangement is brighter?
  • Connect an LED with no resistor. What happens? Now add 220 Ω.
  • Charge a capacitor through a 1 kΩ resistor and watch the voltage creep up.
  • Reverse an LED. Does current flow?
  • Short a battery with a wire and look at the current.
