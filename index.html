Science Studio

Interactive tools for learning by doing: mix chemicals, build circuits and look inside machines. Everything runs in the browser with no installs and no internet connection needed.

⚗️

Virtual Chemistry Lab

Pour acids, bases, salts and metals into a beaker and watch the reactions happen.

  • Neutralisation, precipitation, displacement and gas reactions
  • Live pH meter, thermometer and indicators
  • Titration curve plotted as you add reagent
🔌

Circuit Lab

Describe a circuit in plain words or start from a template, then watch the current flow.

  • Builder: type "three LEDs on a 9 V battery" and it gets wired up
  • 8 project templates and saved projects
  • Batteries, resistors, bulbs, LEDs, switches, capacitors, motors
  • Real nodal analysis solver with live voltages and currents
  • Animated current, glowing bulbs, LEDs that burn out
⚙️

How It Works

Animated, labelled cutaways of everyday machines.

  • Four-stroke engine, DC motor, pin-tumbler lock
  • Play, pause, slow down and scrub through each cycle
  • Step-by-step explanations synced to the animation
